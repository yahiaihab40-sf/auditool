The platform

The full audit lifecycle, in one integrated system.

Six stages that usually live in disconnected spreadsheets, shared folders, and email threads. AUDITool runs them as one standardised, traceable workflow.

  1. 01

    Audit planning

    Build a risk-based annual audit plan, scope each engagement, and allocate resources across the audit universe.

  2. 02

    Risk and control assessment

    Assess inherent and residual risk, and evaluate control design and operating effectiveness, consistently across every engagement.

  3. 03

    Fieldwork and working papers

    Execute audit programmes and keep every working paper and piece of evidence linked to its engagement, with a clear review trail.

  4. 04

    Findings

    Document each observation once, with its risk rating, root cause, owner, and agreed management action plan.

  5. 05

    Corrective-action follow-up

    Track every management action through to closure, with clear ownership, due dates, and status.

  6. 06

    Reporting and dashboards

    Give senior management and the audit committee real-time visibility of audit coverage, open findings, and action status.
