The platform

The full audit lifecycle, in one integrated system.

Six stages that usually live in disconnected spreadsheets, shared folders, and email threads. AUDITool runs them as one standardised, traceable workflow, mapped to the IIA's Global Internal Audit Standards (2024).

  1. 01

    Risk-based audit planning

    Build a risk-based annual audit plan, scope each engagement, and allocate resources across the audit universe.

    GIAS · Standard 9.4
  2. 02

    Risk and control assessment

    Assess inherent and residual risk, and evaluate control design and operating effectiveness, consistently across every engagement.

    GIAS · Standard 13.2
  3. 03

    Audit execution and documentation

    Execute audit programmes, draw samples, and analyse data. Every working paper and piece of evidence stays linked to its engagement, with a clear review trail.

    GIAS · Principle 14
  4. 04

    Findings

    Document each observation once, with its risk rating, root cause, owner, and agreed management action plan.

    GIAS · Principle 14
  5. 05

    Corrective-action follow-up

    Track every management action through to closure, with clear ownership, due dates, and status.

    GIAS · Standard 15.2
  6. 06

    Automated reporting and interactive dashboards

    Reports are generated from the work already in the system, and interactive dashboards give senior management and the audit committee real-time KPIs on coverage, open findings, and action status.

    GIAS · Standard 15.1

Questions buyers ask

Before you book a demo.

How do we move from our existing Excel files?

Migration is part of implementation. Your existing plans, risk registers and open findings are brought into AUDITool, so the team starts with its own history.

How long does implementation take, and how is it priced?

About two weeks from signing. AUDITool is licensed per company, and the price includes implementation. Ask for a quote on the demo call.

Does it handle sampling and data analytics?

Yes. Sampling and data analysis are part of fieldwork, and the results are saved as working papers in the engagement.

Does it integrate with our ERP or Active Directory?

Not today. AUDITool runs as a standalone system and needs no connection to your ERP, which keeps the IT review short.

Can fieldwork be done offline or on a phone?

Not yet. AUDITool runs in the browser on a laptop or desktop and needs an internet connection.

Is AUDITool ISO 27001 certified?

Not yet. The controls listed in the security section are in place today, and we will walk your IT team through them in detail.
